# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they meant well. Say what you mean plainly, and
take a correction the same way you would like one taken.

## What is expected

- Speak to people the way you would want to be spoken to.
- Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that somebody may know something you do not, and that you may know
  something they do not.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps a tally here.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, or comments aimed at somebody rather than their work.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Publishing somebody's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Stiven-Gjekaj/RogueBit/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are read by the
maintainer alone.

Anybody who breaks these rules may have their comments removed, or may be
blocked from the repository.
